How the race works

Your spot on the circle
The route is Denmark's biggest circle, 578 km around. It is split into 360 start spots, one degree apart: 578 km ÷ 360, about 1.6 km between neighbouring spots, measured along the road. Spots are drawn at random once registration closes.
One start, one direction
All riders set off at the same moment and ride the same way around the circle.
Caught means out
When the rider behind you catches you, your race is over. The rider who caught you now chases whoever was ahead of you.
The winner
The last rider still in the race, the one who has caught everyone else, wins.
The ferry
One short crossing of about 100 m is made by a ferry that shuttles back and forth on a fixed schedule. When several riders wait at the bank and cross on the same trip, whoever reached the bank first rolls off with a 2-minute head start on the others, so nobody gets caught just because of ferry timing.

The route

A loop through Jutland, from Aarhus out to the west coast and back. The 360 start spots are spread evenly along the road, one every 1.6 km. Spot 0 is in Aarhus and the numbers count up in the direction of travel.
